For organizations

The missing layer is often identity.

Clinical care, vocational services, access support, and workplace policy all matter. But people also need language for the person they are becoming. Reinvention Works adds that practical identity layer beside the care and services you already provide.

The gap we fill

Treatment addresses the condition. Who helps with what comes next?

The crisis is handled. The services are winding down. And the person is standing in the invisible middle: medically stable, technically “fine,” and quietly lost about what comes next.

The Reinvention Reset™ System is a structured, peer-led next step for exactly that moment, created by Shay Edmonds from his own rebuild after losing his sight to Anti-MOG. It is education that sits beside your care team or service model, never instead of it.

Who we partner with

Three ways organizations use Reinvention Works.

Healthcare providers & care teams

Case managers and discharge planners make the program available as a free-standing resource for stable patients rebuilding identity and direction after diagnosis or functional change. Sharing a resource link requires no health information and no clinical referral.

Vocational rehabilitation & independent living

Return-to-work starts with return-to-self. Vocational rehabilitation offices make the program available as a resource, and independent living centers offer it to the people they serve. It sits beside your services and is built to be doable on a hard day.

Employers & leadership

HR, benefits, and EAP teams share it with employees returning from medical leave or navigating a major life change. Training and speaking for leaders on disability-informed approaches and workplace accommodation are available.

Each partner follows its own compliance and endorsement rules. For publicly funded partners, you can begin simply by making the program available as an independent resource people can choose to use — no formal process required to share it.

Built to work alongside you

The resources you share reflect on your organization. Here is what holds it up.

  • Peer-led education, not therapy. It says so plainly, in the room, and sits beside clinical care.
  • Built for hard days. Live on Zoom, cameras optional, your pace is valid, two of three sessions completes it.
  • Honest about its limits. Anyone in active crisis is directed to care first, out loud.
  • Accessibility is the credential. Designed by and for the disability community. Read our accessibility statement.
  • Privacy first. Partners share a link, never a contact list.
