How to request a refund

Four steps.

  1. Contact us. Email shay@reinventionworksonline.com with "Refund Request" in the subject line.
  2. Provide information. Include your order number, purchase date, the program or product, and a brief reason.
  3. Review. We review requests within 3 business days and may follow up to understand your situation.
  4. Decision and processing. Approved refunds process within 5–7 business days via your original payment method; they typically appear on your statement within 7–10 business days after that.
Special circumstances

The policy is a starting point, not a wall.

We recognize that the disability community faces unique challenges. We consider medical emergencies, capacity crashes (flares, relapses, or periods of significantly reduced capacity), family crises, financial hardship, accessibility barriers on our end, and genuine program mismatch. We can offer extended refund windows, full or partial refunds outside standard eligibility, deferrals to a future cohort, and payment-plan pauses.

We would rather work with you to find a solution than rigidly enforce a policy that does not account for the realities of disability or major life change. Reach out. Most situations can be resolved.

Generally non-refundable

Programs where more than 75% of content has been accessed and completed; individual coaching sessions already delivered; late requests submitted outside the stated window without exceptional circumstances; and third-party payment-processing fees, which are not recoverable by us. Even here, we may make exceptions for extraordinary circumstances. Contact us before assuming a refund is not possible.
