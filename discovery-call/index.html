What to expect

Calls are available Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm Central. You’ll get a confirmation email with your Zoom link straight away.

If you need any accommodation to make the call accessible to you, reply to your confirmation email and Shay will make it work.

Reinvention Works provides peer-led education. It is not therapy, medical care, or crisis support. If you are in crisis, call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency.
