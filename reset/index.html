What this is

A structured reset for the person who remains.

The 3-Day Reinvention Reset™ is a peer-led program for adults rebuilding identity, capacity, and direction after disability, illness, or a health disruption.

It is built for the moment after the crisis has been handled, when the services are winding down and the bigger question is still open: who am I now, and what comes next? Over three live sessions with Shay Edmonds, you work through a practical framework built from lived experience and grounded in research. It sits beside your medical and clinical care and never replaces it.

The 3-day arc

Three days. One direction.

DAY 1

Reality Check

Separate what is factually true from the story layered on top of it, and replace noise with clarity.

DAY 2

Energy Sovereignty

Map your actual capacity and build strategy around what you genuinely have, not what you wish you had.

DAY 3

Recalibrated Action

Build an adaptive forward plan from your actual current position, not a previous self or an expected recovery arc.

What’s included

What you leave with.

  • Three live Zoom sessions, two hours each, over three consecutive days. Cameras are always optional.
  • Reinvention Snapshot — Day 0 and Day 4, so you can see what shifted.
  • Your Next Move, a 60-day bridge delivered when you complete the Reset.
  • A 3-day email support sequence and a resource directory with professional referrals.

Completion means attending two of the three live sessions. Your pace is valid. If you need any accommodation, tell us and we will make it work. How we build for access

How to join

Seats are sponsored by partner organizations.

Reset cohorts run through organizations that support people through life-altering change: vocational rehabilitation offices, independent living centers, care teams, and employers.

  • Have a sponsored seat? Use the private registration link from your sponsoring organization or from Shay.
  • Want the Reset for the people you serve? Bring the Reset to your organization.
  • On your own for now? Start with Your First Move, free, and stay in the loop on new ways to take part.
