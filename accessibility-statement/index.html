Accessibility at Reinvention Works™

This program was built by and for the disability community. Accessibility is not an add-on. It is the foundation.

Our commitment

As a disability-informed company, accessibility is not just a legal requirement, it is a core value. This work was designed by and for the disability community, led by founder Shay Edmonds. We believe everyone deserves access to identity-reconstruction resources, and we are committed to making our website, programs, and services accessible to people with diverse abilities, assistive technologies, and lived realities.

This statement reflects our ongoing efforts to ensure ReinventionWorksOnline.com is usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology, have cognitive or sensory disabilities, or need flexible access options.

Standards

We follow WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

01

Perceivable

Information can be perceived by all senses.

02

Operable

Interface elements are usable by everyone.

03

Understandable

Information and interface operation are clear.

04

Robust

Content works across diverse technologies.

What we build in

Website accessibility features.

01

Keyboard navigation

Full site functionality by keyboard alone, with logical tab order and visible focus indicators.

02

Screen reader support

Semantic HTML, landmarks, alt text on every image, and descriptive link text.

03

Visual accessibility

Contrast meeting WCAG 2.1 AA, scalable text, and color-independent design.

04

Cognitive accessibility

Clear navigation, consistent layout, plain language, and no decorative noise.

05

Motor accessibility

Large click targets and generous spacing. No time-limited interactions unless truly necessary.

06

Motion & sensory sensitivity

No autoplay audio or video. Minimal, purposeful animation. Reduced-motion preference respected.

Live program accessibility

3-Day Reinvention Reset™

Platform: Zoom. Session structure: designed for energy variability. Cameras are optional. Rest is not failure. Completion means attending two of the three live sessions, so a hard day does not cost you your spot.

Materials: visible labels rather than placeholder-only form fields, and descriptive error messages announced to screen readers.

Known limitations

Honest about where we are.

We are actively working on enhanced captioning and transcripts for recorded content, and improved form error messaging. If you hit a barrier we have not listed, tell us. The people this was built for are part of how we evaluate it.

Report an accessibility issue

Contact Shay Edmonds directly.

Include the page or feature involved, the assistive technology you used if any, your browser and operating system, and what you were trying to do. We acknowledge within 24 hours, assess within 3 business days, and aim for a workaround or fix timeline within 5 business days.

Email shay@reinventionworksonline.com
