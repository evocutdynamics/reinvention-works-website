The founder

A company built on one person's rebuild.

Shay Edmonds was a 911 emergency communications engineer building systems that kept people safe at their worst moments. Then Anti-MOG, a rare autoimmune disease, attacked his optic nerves and took his sight, his career, and, for a while, his sense of who he was.

Traditional therapy told him to accept a new normal. Motivational speakers told him to overcome. What he needed were practical tools that worked with his actual capacity, and a community that did not demand performance. When he could not find them, he built them. That work became Reinvention Works.

Mission and vision

Your reality is the foundation, not the obstacle.

To support adults navigating identity collapse after disability and health disruption, with honest, practical tools that honor their reality and rebuild what actually matters.
