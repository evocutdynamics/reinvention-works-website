About the speaker

Shay Edmonds

A former 911 emergency communications engineer who lost his sight to Anti-MOG and rebuilt his identity, career, and independence.

Shay brings a perspective few speakers can: lived disability experience alongside professional training in psychology, integrative nutrition, and health coaching, and experience supporting hundreds of people through individual and group coaching.
