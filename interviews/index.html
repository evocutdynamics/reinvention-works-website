Interviews & podcast

Interviews and podcast appearances.

Reinvention Works founder Shay Edmonds talks honestly about losing his sight, rebuilding who he is, and how Reinvention Works helps people start again after disability or a major health change.

Together We Rise · with Rochelle and Alisha Schwartz · November 2026

If Your Life Were a Movie, What Would This Chapter Be Called?

How a major life disruption affects identity, relationships and confidence, and what you can build from where you are now. For anyone whose life has been upended and who is asking who they are now.

Originally recorded for Together We Rise with Rochelle and Alisha Schwartz. Full episode: "If Your Life Were a Movie, What Would This Chapter Be Called?" on YouTube (opens in new tab)

Find Your Way on New Cleveland Radio · with Karen Hale and Kristy Anderson · November 19, 2026

Reinvent Yourself with Shay

On this show about grief: rebuilding a life after disability and a major disruption, and the question all the medical care never asked: who am I going to be now? Helpful for anyone grieving a life that has changed, and for the families beside them.

Originally recorded for Find Your Way on New Cleveland Radio with Karen Hale and Kristy Anderson. Full episode: "Reinvent Yourself with Shay" on YouTube (opens in new tab)

Famous Interviews · with Joe Dimino · November 17, 2026

Shay Edmonds on Reinvention, Overcoming Life Challenges and Stopping Self-Sabotage

Losing most of your vision as an adult, and the gap between treatment and building a life you want afterwards. Also covers the book Stop the Self Sabotage and the patterns that hold people back. For anyone whose life has taken an unexpected turn.

Originally recorded for Famous Interviews with Joe Dimino. Full episode: "Shay Edmonds on Reinvention, Overcoming Life Challenges and Stopping Self-Sabotage" on YouTube (opens in new tab)

The Inclusive Dad Podcast · with Aaron DeVries · November 10, 2026

Episode 226: Your Disability Changed Your Life. It Didn't End Your Possibilities!

Losing independence to vision loss, and how it touches relationships, dating, parenting and learning to ask for help. Also, how the stories we tell ourselves can keep us playing small. Helpful for people rebuilding after disability, and for families who want to understand inclusion.

Originally recorded for The Inclusive Dad Podcast with Aaron DeVries. Full episode: "Episode 226: Your Disability Changed Your Life. It Didn't End Your Possibilities!" on YouTube (opens in new tab)

Reclaim Your Energy After 50 · with John Curran · November 3, 2026

When the Old You No Longer Fits: Reinventing Yourself and Finding What's Next

Becoming legally blind and rethinking independence, career and relationships, and letting go of self-sabotaging beliefs. For people over 50 facing a life transition or wondering what comes next.

Originally recorded for Reclaim Your Energy After 50 with John Curran. Full episode: "When the Old You No Longer Fits: Reinventing Yourself and Finding What's Next" on YouTube (opens in new tab)

Healthy Mind, Healthy Life · with Avik · October 30, 2026

Rebuilding Identity After Life Disruption: You Are Not Broken, You Are Unfinished

The question that arrives when the appointments end: who am I now? Why healing the body and rebuilding a sense of self are two different journeys, and how a new blueprint and small daily steps help. For anyone whose life has been rewritten by a health change or disability.

Originally recorded for Healthy Mind, Healthy Life with Avik. Full episode: "Rebuilding Identity After Life Disruption: You Are Not Broken, You Are Unfinished" on YouTube (opens in new tab)
