What is Your First Move?

Your First Move is a free guide from Reinvention Works™ for adults navigating identity change after disability, illness, or a health disruption. It holds three short exercises: the Story Separator, the Energy Snapshot, and One True Thing. Together they help you separate fact from story, plan from your real capacity, and find one true anchor to build from. It is peer-informed and educational, not clinical.

Who it’s for

Built for the gap nobody hands you a map for.

  • For you if you’re past the acute phase of a disability, illness, or health disruption, and the words recovery, resilience and silver linings don’t describe what you’re living.
  • For you if every planning tool you’ve tried assumed you had consistent, predictable energy.
  • Not a replacement for clinical care, therapy, or crisis support.
A note from Shay

I built this because I lived in the gap.

Every tool I found assumed I had consistent energy. Every program assumed I was building back toward something recognizable. Every support group processed what was lost but didn’t build what came next.

For years I searched for a framework that addressed identity after life-changing disruption. I never found one. So I built one.

Shay Edmonds, Founder, Reinvention Works™ · Read Shay’s story
